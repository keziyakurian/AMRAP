Scope of Work (SOW) - Brand Health Tracker 2026

Objectives:
The study aims to track the brand health of key players in the Smartphone category.
Key KPIs to be monitored include:
- Top of Mind Awareness (TOMA)
- Spontaneous Awareness
- Aided Brand Awareness
- Brand Consideration (Top 2 Box)
- Usage in the last 6 months
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)

Imagery Attributes:
- "A brand I trust"
- "Good value for money"
- "Innovative technology"
- "Stylish design"

Target Audience:
- Males and Females
- Aged 18-45
- Nationwide coverage

Methodology:
- Quantitative Online Survey
- N=500 per wave
